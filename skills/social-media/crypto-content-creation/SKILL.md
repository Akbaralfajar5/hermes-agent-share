## Interrupt Pattern Library

```
Gap:        "What [audience] doesn't know about [domain]..."
Reframe:    "Stop [accepted behavior]. Here's what works."
Proof:      "I did [outcome] in [window] using this:"
Method:     "How to [goal] without [friction]"
Index:      "[N] reasons [audience] fails at [goal]"
Arc:        "[Time ago] I was [state]. Today: [contrast]."
Position:   "Unpopular opinion: [defensible contrarian claim]"
Alert:      "If you're doing [behavior], stop. Here's why:"
Lossfear:   "[Audience] losing [thing] because of [unseen cause]"
Validate:   "[Specific number] of [audience] confirmed: [insight]"
```

## Format Specifications

### Short-form video (60–90s — TikTok, Reels, Shorts)

```
0–3s:    interrupt signal (hook + visual change)
3–15s:   qualification / context
15–50s:  core value delivery (numbered if 3+ points)
50–60s:  engagement directive (comment / follow / save)
```

### Twitter/X thread (CT-style, crypto-aware)

```
Tweet 1: Interrupt (no thread emoji on tweet 1, controversial works)
Tweet 2–8: One proof point each (max 280 chars, keep punchy)
Tweet 9: Synthesis / "tl;dr"
Tweet 10: Directive (RT + follow + bookmark)
```

### Carousel (IG / LinkedIn)

```
Slide 1: Title + hook
Slide 2: Pain point
Slides 3–7: Method (one per slide)
Slide 8: Recap
Slide 9: CTA (save + share)
```

## Indonesian Voice Patterns (CT / kripto / casual)

```
- Pakai "lo/gue" untuk CT vibe, "kalian" untuk audience yang lebih luas
- Slank kripto: gas, cuan, sultan, FOMO, ngegas, langsung gas, bagi-bagi, sat-set
- Hindari: hari yang cerah, semoga membantu, salam hangat (formal banget)
- Pakai "btw", "ngomong-ngomong", "fyi" untuk transition
- Pertanyaan retoris di akhir: "Lo udah join belum?" / "Mau ikutan?"
```

## Engagement Question Patterns (Non-Crypto, Indonesian Twitter)

When user wants to create engaging posts/questions that get lots of replies — especially personal, relatable "man to man" style questions.

### Proven Formats

**1. The Honest Number Drop**
> "Berapa umur lo, dan berapa tabungan lo sekarang? Jujur aja."

Why it works: People love sharing numbers anonymously. Low effort to reply, high relatability.

**2. The Unpopular Opinion**
> "Gw rasa 90% orang yg bilang 'kerja keras' itu sebenernya cuma ga punya strategi — setuju?"

Why it works: Forces people to pick a side. Pro/kontra = massive replies.

**3. The Storytelling Bait**
> "Cerita satu hal paling bodoh yg lo pernah pas muda dan orangtua lo sampe sekarang ga tau."

Why it works: Everyone has a story. Low barrier, high entertainment value.

**4. The Plot Twist Parent Angle**
> "Dulu gw pikir gw pinter nyembunyiin hal dari nyokap. Sekarang umur 24, gw sadar... dia tau semuanya. Dia cuma milih diem."

Why it works: Emotional + relatable + universal experience. Triggers "omg sama" responses.

**5. The Either/Or Dilemma**
> "Gaji 10jt/bulan vs freelance 20jt/bulan ga tentu — lo pilih mana dan kenapa?"

Why it works: No wrong answer. People love explaining their choices.

**6. The Quarter-Life Crisis Reflection**
> "Umur 24. Makin banyak pikiran, makin sering menyendiri. Bukan karena sedang — tapi karena mulai pilih siapa yg worth waktu gw."

Why it works: Gen Z/millennial mass relatable. Shareable = "ini gw banget".

### Viral Engagement Ranking (by reply rate)
1. **Honest number drop** — easiest to reply, anonymous but personal
2. **Unpopular opinion** — forces debate, high reply count
3. **Storytelling bait** — entertainment value, people love sharing
4. **Parent/family angle** — emotional, universal, shareable
5. **Either/or dilemma** — low effort reply, sparks discussion
6. **Quarter-life reflection** — relatable, but more "like" than "reply"

### Key Rules for Engagement Questions
- ONE question per post (not multiple)
- No hashtags (looks desperate)
- No emojis in the question itself (save for follow-up)
- Post at peak hours (12-14 WIB lunch, 19-22 WIB evening)
- First reply should be your own answer (shows vulnerability, encourages others)
- Indonesian casual register (lo/gue, gw, jujur, sih, dong)

## Constraints

- Minimum 3 interrupt variations when generating hooks
- Always specify platform AND format AND length
- Every output includes engagement directive — no exceptions
- Indonesian content: avoid Google-translate phrasing, use natural slang
- Crypto content: include risk note when shilling ("DYOR", "high risk", or stronger)
