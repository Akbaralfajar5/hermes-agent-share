# TOEFL Reading Comprehension — Gist & Focus Questions

Pattern reference for TOEFL-style reading passages. User practices with printed test pages (photos/scans). Always answer in Indonesian with casual lo/gue style.

## Question Types (Gist/Focus)

### 1. Topic / Subject
**Ask pattern:** "Which best describes the topic/subject of the passage?"
**What to find:** Pokok bahasan utama — apa yang dibahas secara keseluruhan
**Method:** Eliminate options yang terlalu spesifik (cuma satu detail) atau terlalu umum (ga spesifik ke isi passage)
**Trap:** Detail-heavy options yang cuma mewakili 1 paragraf

### 2. Main Idea
**Ask pattern:** "The main idea of this passage is that..." / "Which best states the main idea?"
**What to find:** Gagasan utama — kesimpulan/pesan yang ingin disampaikan penulis
**Method:** Cari ide yang **berulang di seluruh passage**, bukan cuma di satu kalimat
**Trap:** Options yang benar secara fakta tapi bukan IDE UTAMA (hanya supporting detail)

### 3. Best Title
**Ask pattern:** "Which would be the best title for this passage?"
**What to find:** Judul yang merangkum SELURUH isi passage
**Method:** Judul harus mencakup topik + konteks, bukan terlalu spesifik atau terlalu umum
**Trap:** Judul yang catchy tapi ga akurat, atau judul yang cuma cover sebagian

### 4. Subject of Paragraph X
**Ask pattern:** "The subject of the [Nth] paragraph is..."
**What to find:** Subjek utama paragraf spesifik
**Method:** Lihat **mana yang paling banyak dibahas** (paling banyak kalimat/detail) di paragraf itu
**Trap:** Mengira kalimat terakhir = subjek (padahal cuma penutup/kesimpulan)

## Response Format

```
## Jawaban & Penjelasan

**Passage:** [1-2 kalimat ringkasan dalam bahasa Indonesia]

---

**Soal [N]** — *[type]* (penjelasan type dalam Indo)

**Jawaban: (X) [full answer text]**

Penjelasan: [1-2 kalimat kenapa ini benar, refer ke isi passage]

- (A) [kenapa salah — singkat]
- (B) [kenapa salah — singkat]
- (C) [kenapa salah — singpak]
- (D) [kenapa salah — singkat]
```

Rules:
- Answer EXACT text from options (don't paraphrase)
- Explain ALL options (why right + why wrong)
- Reference specific parts of passage as evidence
- Keep explanations concise (2-3 sentences max per option)
- Indonesian, casual but clear

## Elimination Strategy (teach to user)

1. **Baca pertanyaan DULU** — tau yang dicari apa sebelum baca passage
2. **Skim passage** — cari ide utama tiap paragraf
3. **Eliminate yang jelas salah** — biasanya 1-2 option langsung keliatan salah
4. **Antara 2 sisa** — pilih yang lebih **broad** (mencakup keseluruhan) bukan yang lebih specific

## Common Traps

| Trap | Contoh | Kenapa salah |
|------|--------|--------------|
| Too specific | "The hardness of diamonds" | Cuma 1 detail dari passage |
| Too general | "The city of Fort Knox" | Ga spesifik ke apa yang dibahas |
| Opposite | "People did not enjoy his poetry" | Kebalikan dari isi passage |
| Not mentioned | "The Damage and Destruction" | Ga ada di passage sama sekali |
| Partial | "Fort Knox Security" | Cuma sebagian kecil dari passage |

## Answering Pattern for Multiple Questions per Passage

When passage has 3+ questions (like Passage Four with Q7-9):
- Q7-Q8 usually gist/focus (topic, main idea, title)
- Q9+ usually more specific (subject of paragraph X, detail, inference)
- For "subject of paragraph" — focus ONLY on that paragraph, not the whole passage
- For "main idea" — consider ALL paragraphs, find the recurring theme
