# Pump.fun Token Screening Guide

Comprehensive checklist and strategy for screening meme tokens on Pump.fun to avoid rug pulls and scams.

## Quick Screening Checklist

Before buying any token, verify:

- [ ] Creator wallet history (no rug pulls)
- [ ] Initial buy < 5 SOL
- [ ] Market cap $5K-$50K
- [ ] Top 10 holders < 40% supply
- [ ] Active Twitter/Telegram
- [ ] Volume 24h > 20% market cap
- [ ] Chart rises gradually (not vertical)
- [ ] Holder count > 100
- [ ] Token age > 1 hour
- [ ] Strong narrative/meme

**Pass 7/10 → Worth entry | < 5/10 → Skip**

## Risk Categories

### 1. Rug Pull / Developer Dump
**Pattern:** Creator buys large amount at launch, pumps price, dumps everything.

**Detection:**
- Initial buy > 5-10 SOL (too large)
- Creator wallet has history of abandoned tokens
- Token pumps 100-500% in 1-2 minutes then crashes

**Tools:**
- Solscan.io → check creator wallet history
- Rugcheck.xyz → automated rug detection
- Birdeye.so → holder distribution

### 2. Pump & Dump Groups
**Pattern:** Coordinated buying → artificial pump → coordinated selling.

**Detection:**
- Volume spike with no catalyst
- Many wallets buy at same time (same amounts)
- Price rises vertically then crashes in minutes

### 3. Low Liquidity Trap
**Pattern:** Token with low volume → can't sell without massive slippage.

**Detection:**
- Market cap < $5K
- Volume 24h < $1K
- Holder count < 50
- Slippage > 30% on exit

### 4. Copycat / Fake Tokens
**Pattern:** Token mimics popular project, claims to be "official".

**Detection:**
- Name similar but has typo (BONK vs B0NK)
- Description copy-pasted from other token
- No social media or website
- Recently created contract

## Entry Timing

### Best Entry Windows

**30 minutes - 2 hours after launch:**
- Price discovery complete
- Scams/rugs usually visible by now
- Still early enough for profit
- Volume and holder count established

**Avoid:**
- < 5 minutes after launch (too risky, no data)
- > 12 hours after launch (momentum gone)
- During obvious pump (wait for pullback)

### Entry Checklist
1. Token passes screening (7/10 criteria)
2. Chart shows healthy pullback (not at top)
3. Volume is consistent (not spiking/dying)
4. Holder count growing steadily
5. Social media has real activity

## Position Sizing & Risk Management

### Portfolio Allocation
- **Max 2-5% per token** (never all-in)
- **Max 10-20% total** in pump.fun trading
- **Rest in stable assets** (SOL, BTC, stablecoins)

### Daily Limits
- Max 3-5 tokens per day
- Max 10 SOL total exposure
- Stop trading after 3 consecutive losses

### Position Entry
- Start with 50% of planned position
- Add remaining 50% if thesis confirms
- Never average down on losers

## Exit Strategy

### Take Profit Levels
- **TP1 (50-100%):** Sell 30-50% of position
- **TP2 (200-300%):** Sell 30% more
- **TP3 (500%+):** Sell remaining or trail stop

### Stop Loss
- **Hard stop:** -30% from entry (cut fast)
- **Trailing stop:** After 100% profit, move stop to breakeven
- **Time stop:** No profit in 24h → exit

### Exit Rules
- ✅ Take profit in stages (don't be greedy)
- ✅ Cut losses quickly (don't hope)
- ✅ Never average down
- ✅ Exit if narrative breaks (social media dies, volume drops)

## Common Mistakes

### 1. FOMO Buying
**Mistake:** Buying at top because "everyone is making money"
**Fix:** Wait for pullback, stick to entry criteria

### 2. Holding Losers
**Mistake:** -50% loss, "it will come back"
**Fix:** Cut at -30%, move on to next opportunity

### 3. No Position Sizing
**Mistake:** All-in on one token
**Fix:** 2-5% max per token, diversify

### 4. Ignoring Red Flags
**Mistake:** "This one is different" despite 5 red flags
**Fix:** If it fails screening, skip it

### 5. Revenge Trading
**Mistake:** Lost money, immediately enter next token to "make it back"
**Fix:** Stop trading after 3 losses, review what went wrong
