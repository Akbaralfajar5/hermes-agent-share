# English Learning Quick-Start Checklist

## This Week (Setup Phase)

- [ ] Download Anki (anki.download)
- [ ] Install Anki deck: "English 1000 Most Common Words"
- [ ] Subscribe to YouTube: English Addict with Mr. Duncan
- [ ] Get graded reader: Penguin Readers Level 1 (or search for free PDF)
- [ ] Create Google Sheets vocabulary log (columns: Date | Word | Part of Speech | Example Sentence | Notes)
- [ ] Test recording: Use phone voice memo or Audacity to record yourself reading

**Time investment:** ~30 minutes setup

---

## Week 1 Routine (1 hour)

**Before the session (daily, 5-10 min):**
- Review Anki cards (10-15 new cards/day)

**Weekly session (1 hour):**
- **15 min:** Anki — learn 20 new words
- **20 min:** Watch 1 English Addict video (watch 2x: first time listen, second time read subtitles)
- **15 min:** Read 5 pages of Penguin Readers Level 1
- **10 min:** Record yourself reading 1 paragraph (1-2 min), listen back

## Pitfalls to Avoid

❌ **Don't skip Anki reviews** — vocabulary won't stick without spaced repetition
❌ **Don't translate everything** — you'll get stuck in translation mode
❌ **Don't watch passively** — pause, repeat, write down new words
❌ **Don't skip speaking** — reading alone won't make you fluent
❌ **Don't switch methods every week** — stick with one approach for 4-8 weeks
❌ **Don't expect fast results** — 1 hour/week = slow but steady (1-2 years to fluency)

## If You Get Stuck

**Problem:** Anki feels boring
**Solution:** Add images to cards, use context sentences instead of isolated words, or switch to "English 500 Most Common Words" (shorter deck, faster wins)

**Problem:** Can't understand video even with subtitles
**Solution:** Watch videos you've already seen before (YouTube algorithm), or find videos on topics you know well (food, sports, tech)

**Problem:** Don't have time for 1 hour/week
**Solution:** Do 15 min Anki daily (5 min) + 10 min reading (3x/week) instead. Consistency > intensity.

**Problem:** Feeling discouraged after 4 weeks
**Solution:** Record yourself now, compare with week 4. You WILL hear improvement. Progress is real even if it feels slow.
